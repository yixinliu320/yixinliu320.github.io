Introduction#

This course introduces the fundamental concepts and techniques for managing government and nonprofit agencies. It focuses on structural models; individual behavior, including group dynamics and leadership; effective use and management of human resources; political and cultural frameworks; and interorganizational relations and networks within the context of current theory and application. Questions of effectiveness, responsibility, and professional relations are considered. Topics will cover contemporary management theory; the nature of complex organizations; organizational and intergovernmental arrangements; roles and responsibilities of managers; managerial and organizational effectiveness; organizational structure and dynamics.
